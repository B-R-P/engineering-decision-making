# Divergence and local minima

Load when breadth is **Broad** or **Forced-diverse**, or when the leading option is merely the first adequate one.

## The trap

You are in a local minimum when your recent candidates are reworkings of the same idea, or when the first adequate answer ended the search. The cause is interleaved generation and evaluation. Separate them: generate broadly without judging, then evaluate the survivors.

## Moves

**Constraint ablation.** Remove each *soft* constraint in turn and ask what solution appears. The best answers often live behind a constraint nobody questioned. A market price or an industry practice is not a law of physics.

**Blank-slate reconstruction.** Ask: "Knowing nothing about the current solution, what would I build from the hard constraints alone?" Rebuild; do not polish the inherited form.

**Altitude shift.** Solve one level up ("do we need this component at all?") and one level down ("what is the simplest primitive that achieves the outcome?"). Most local minima sit at the wrong altitude.

**Solution families.** Enumerate candidates across different classes — eliminate, simplify, substitute, combine, reverse, change the data model, change the interface, change the process, buy, do nothing. Three rewrites of one idea are one candidate.

**Verbalized sampling.** Ask for several candidate approaches and their probabilities, then surface the strongest. A request for "creative ideas" collapses to one mode; a request for a distribution preserves the tail. This is the concrete form of tail sampling — deliberately surfacing the low-probability, high-upside candidates a single answer would miss.

**Dedupe.** Reject candidates that are rewordings of one you already hold. When most of a round is duplicates, diversity has collapsed — converge.

**Inversion.** Design how to *guarantee* failure, then avoid it. Failure is easier to enumerate than success.

**Cross-domain transplant.** Ask how a field without your conventions solves this (biology, logistics, games, manufacturing). Novel solutions cluster at domain edges.

**10x / obsolescence.** Ask what a 10x better or 10x cheaper solution looks like, and what would make the current choice irrelevant. This breaks incrementalism.

**Dual-track.** Run the analysis twice under different framings, or dispatch an independent pass, and compare. Agreement is confidence; disagreement localizes the real uncertainty.

## Stopping

Bound divergence to a fixed budget — one focused pass or a set number of candidates — then converge on evidence. One adequate option is a reason to stop at **Narrow** breadth, not at **Forced-diverse**.
